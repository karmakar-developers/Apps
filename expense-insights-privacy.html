Privacy Policy

Privacy Policy for Expense Insights Android Application
Application: Expense Insights (Android) • Package: com.karmakardevelopers.expenseinsights • Last Updated: September 5, 2026
Privacy Commitment: At Expense Insights, built by Karmakar Developers, protecting your personal and financial information is our highest priority. This Privacy Policy details our practices in strict adherence to Google Play Developer Program Policies (including the User Data and Data Safety requirements).

1. Overview and Core Philosophy

Expense Insights is a personal financial management application designed to help individuals and families track expenditures, categorize income, manage account balances, set budgets, and derive meaningful analytics.

We operate on a Privacy-First architecture. Your financial records, transaction entries, bank/account names, notes, and budgets are stored locally on your device in an encrypted local database. We do not sell, rent, monetize, or broker your personal or financial data to third parties under any circumstances.

2. Information We Collect and Process

In accordance with Google Play's User Data policy, we provide clear disclosure of all data collected, accessed, or processed:

  • Financial Information (Local Storage): Transaction amounts, dates, notes, categories, payment methods, account titles, and budget thresholds entered by you. This data remains on your local device.
  • Authentication Data (Optional): If you choose to sign in via Google Sign-In, we receive your basic profile credentials (name, email address, and profile photo URL) via Firebase Authentication to authenticate your identity.
  • Audio Data (Optional & Ephemeral): If you utilize our voice input feature (Maya Voice Assistant) to record a transaction by voice, speech is processed in real time via the Android system Speech Recognizer. Audio data is not retained, stored on external servers, or repurposed.
  • Device & App Performance Data: Standard anonymous diagnostics, crash logs, and non-identifiable app interaction statistics to maintain stability and prevent crashes.

3. Android System Permissions & Purpose

Expense Insights requests only the minimal permissions required to deliver its core features:

Permission Type Justification & User Benefit
RECORD_AUDIO Runtime (Dangerous) Enables voice-to-text input for hands-free transaction logging when you tap the microphone button. Audio is processed ephemerally and never saved.
POST_NOTIFICATIONS Runtime (Dangerous) Allows daily expense reminder notifications and budget threshold alerts configured by you.
USE_BIOMETRIC / USE_FINGERPRINT Normal Allows optional App Lock biometric authentication using your device's secure hardware enclave. Biometric data never leaves your device.
INTERNET & ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE Normal Enables Google Sign-In, Firebase services, and AI assistance features.
SCHEDULE_EXACT_ALARM & RECEIVE_BOOT_COMPLETED Normal Restores your custom reminder schedule after device restarts.
CAMERA Optional Feature Used strictly if you capture receipt photos for transaction attachments. Never used in the background.

4. How We Use Your Information

We process collected information exclusively for the following specified purposes:

  • Providing personal financial analytics, monthly cash flow summaries, and category charts.
  • Sending scheduled reminder notifications according to your customized settings.
  • Facilitating secure CSV export and import backups initiated by you.
  • Authenticating your account via Google Identity Services if you choose to sign in.
  • Troubleshooting bugs, app crashes, and improving application responsiveness.

We do not use your financial data for advertising, behavioural targeting, or credit profiling.

5. Third-Party Service Providers

We partner only with industry-standard, secure infrastructure providers that comply with Google Play Developer policies:

  • Google Play Services: Used for in-app updates, licensing, and device framework integration.
  • Google Identity / Firebase Authentication: Provides token-based authentication when using Google Sign-In. Governed by Google's Privacy Policy.
  • Google Gemini API: Provides natural-language financial queries, automated categorization, and intelligent budgeting suggestions. Queries do not train foundation models on user-specific personal data without consent.

6. Google Play Data Safety Declarations

For complete transparency in the Google Play Store Data Safety section:

  • Data Shared: We do NOT share your personal or financial data with any third-party advertisers, data brokers, or external commercial entities.
  • Data Encrypted in Transit: All communications between the app and external services (e.g., Firebase or Gemini API) use industry-standard HTTPS / TLS 1.3 encryption.
  • Data Encrypted at Rest: Application database storage is sandboxed within your Android OS user partition.

7. Data Retention & Your Deletion Rights

In accordance with Google Play's Account and Data Deletion Policy, you maintain complete ownership and control over your data:

  • In-App Data Reset: You can wipe all accounts, transactions, and categories at any time by navigating to Settings > Reset App & Restart Setup.
  • Account Deletion: You can request permanent account and remote data deletion directly within the application via Settings > Delete Account, or online at our dedicated portal: https://karmakar-developers.netlify.app/#data-center.
  • Email Deletion Request: You may also email developers.karmakar@gmail.com with the subject line "Data Deletion Request". All associated authentication records and cloud-linked data will be permanently purged within 30 days.

8. Children's Privacy (COPPA Compliance)

Expense Insights is intended for general audiences aged 13 and above (or 16 and above in the European Economic Area). We do not knowingly collect, solicit, or store personal information from children under 13. If we discover that a child under 13 has provided us with personal data, we will take immediate steps to delete that data from our records.

9. Security Measures

We employ comprehensive technical and organizational measures to safeguard your information:

  • Biometric lock security integration (Fingerprint and Face Unlock) via Android BiometricPrompt API.
  • Encrypted transport protocols (TLS/SSL) for all network endpoints.
  • Strict app sandboxing preventing unauthorized third-party apps on your device from reading database contents.

10. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy periodically to reflect changes in our legal obligations, features, or Google Play store policy updates. When changes are made, we will update the "Last Updated" date at the top of this document. We encourage you to review this Privacy Policy periodically.
