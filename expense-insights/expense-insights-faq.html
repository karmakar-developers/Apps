Payment & Purchase

Lifetime product details, Google Play billing, pending orders, and status verification

15 Questions
1 What am I purchasing?
expand_more

You are purchasing Expense Insights Pro Lifetime. It provides lifetime access to the Pro features included with the purchase. There is no recurring subscription for this product.

2 How is my payment processed?
expand_more

Payments are securely processed through Google Play Billing. Expense Insights does not directly process or store your payment card or banking details.

3 When will my Pro access become active?
expand_more

Your Pro access becomes active after Google Play confirms that your purchase has been successfully completed.

4 My payment is showing as Pending. What does this mean?
expand_more

A pending payment means Google Play has not yet completed the purchase. Pro access will remain inactive until Google Play confirms the purchase as completed.

5 What should I do if my payment is pending?
expand_more

Please wait for Google Play to complete the payment. You can use the Check Pending Payment option in the Expense Insights Pro screen to check the status again.

6 Can I use Pro features while my payment is pending?
expand_more

No. Pro access remains inactive while the payment is pending. Once Google Play confirms the purchase, your Pro access can be activated.

7 I was charged, but Pro access is not showing. What should I do?
expand_more

First, make sure you are signed in with the same Google account/email associated with your Expense Insights purchase. Then use the Restore option or Check Pending Payment option where applicable. The app checks your purchase information and Google Play purchase status to restore access.

8 What happens if I reinstall the app or change my device?
expand_more

Your purchase can be restored when you sign in with the account associated with your purchase. The app checks the available purchase and entitlement information to restore eligible Pro access.

9 Do I need to purchase Pro again after reinstalling the app?
expand_more

If you already purchased the lifetime Pro product, you should not need to purchase it again. Use the Restore option after signing in with the relevant account.

10 Is Expense Insights Pro a subscription?
expand_more

No. The product is a lifetime purchase. The purchase flow treats the product as lifetime access rather than an expiring subscription.

11 Will my Pro access expire?
expand_more

The Pro product is configured as a lifetime purchase, so there is no regular subscription expiration date associated with it.

12 What happens if my payment fails or is cancelled?
expand_more

If Google Play does not report the purchase as completed or pending, Pro access remains inactive and the purchase screen allows you to try again.

13 Can I buy Pro again if a previous payment failed?
expand_more

Yes. If the previous purchase was not completed, the Buy Now option remains available so you can initiate another purchase.

14 How can I check whether my purchase was successful?
expand_more

After a successful Google Play purchase, Expense Insights activates Pro access. You can also use the restore or pending-payment checking options when applicable.

15 Does Expense Insights store my card or bank details?
expand_more

No. Payment processing is handled through Google Play. Expense Insights uses purchase information returned by Google Play to verify the purchase and provide access.

Refunds & Chargebacks

Google Play refund requests, chargeback handling, and administrative entitlements

3 Questions
16 How do I request a refund?
expand_more

Refunds are handled through Google Play's purchase and refund process. Please use the Google Play purchase associated with your transaction to check the available refund options.

17 What happens if a payment is refunded or charged back?
expand_more

Refund and chargeback situations are handled through the app's administrative purchase-management process. Access may be updated based on the resulting purchase status.

18 Can Expense Insights manually provide or remove Pro access?
expand_more

Access can also be granted administratively in certain cases. The app recognizes an administrative entitlement in the same access-checking flow.

Google Account & Purchase

Account matching, device migration, email identity, and multi-account sign-in

3 Questions
19 Which account should I use when purchasing?
expand_more

Use the Google account that you want associated with your Google Play purchase and Expense Insights access.

20 I signed in with a different account. Can I still restore my purchase?
expand_more

The purchase restoration process checks the signed-in user's account information against the purchase records. If the purchase belongs to another account, sign in with the account associated with that purchase.

21 Why does the app need my email address?
expand_more

Your signed-in email is used to associate your Expense Insights purchase with your account so that your Pro entitlement can be identified and restored.

Referral Program

Sharing referral codes, milestone rewards, and activating Premium access

10 Questions
22 How does the Referral Program work?
expand_more

Share Expense Insights and your referral code with friends. When your friends install the app and successfully add your referral code, they count as successful referrals.

23 Where does my friend enter my referral code?
expand_more

Your friend can enter your referral code from Settings → Refer & Unlock Premium.

24 When is a referral counted?
expand_more

A referral is counted after your friend installs Expense Insights and successfully adds your referral code.

25 How do I activate my Premium reward?
expand_more

Once you reach a reward milestone, open Refer & Unlock Premium and tap Check & Activate Premium.

26 What Premium rewards can I unlock?
expand_more
  • 5 Friends → 1 Month Premium
  • 10 Friends → 6 Months Premium
  • 15 Friends → 1 Year Premium
  • 20 Friends → Lifetime Premium
27 Are the rewards cumulative?
expand_more

No. Your reward is based on the highest referral milestone you have reached.

28 Can I use my own referral code?
expand_more

No. Self-referrals are not allowed.

29 Can the same friend be counted more than once?
expand_more

No. Each friend/account can contribute only one successful referral.

30 Can I change my referral code after adding one?
expand_more

No. Once a referral code has been successfully added to an account, it cannot be changed.

31 When will my Premium be activated?
expand_more

After reaching an eligible milestone, tap Check & Activate Premium. If the referral requirements are met, the applicable Premium reward will be activated on your account.

Still need assistance with your payment?

Contact our support team directly. We are happy to help verify your purchase or resolve entitlement questions.
